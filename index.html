Play Tiến Lên Miền Nam online

VC13.gg is a free browser game of Tiến Lên, also written Tien Len and known as the Vietnamese 13-card game or Thirteen. Join a multiplayer game with Quick Play, or create a room and invite friends.

How to play Tiến Lên

Four players each receive 13 cards. Play singles, pairs, triples, or consecutive runs to beat the cards on the table. The first player to get rid of every card wins. VC13.gg follows the Southern Vietnamese rules, Tiến Lên Miền Nam. Open seats in multiplayer games are filled by bots.
